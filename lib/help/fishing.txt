Fishing
=======

Stand beside eligible water and press Ctrl+C (Cast).  Fishing opens across the
playfield while leaving the surrounding world visible.  Opening and cancelling
are free; every rod movement, line movement, wait, strike and reel is a normal
turn.  Creatures and hazards therefore keep acting while you fish.

Waiting for a bite
------------------

Left / keypad 4      extend the rod one column
Right / keypad 6     retract the rod one column
Down / keypad 2      lower the hook one depth row
Up / keypad 8        raise the hook one depth row
Space / keypad 5     hold position for one turn
Escape               stop fishing

Fish move before a stationary hook can receive a bite.  Moving the hook cannot
also cause a bite on that turn.  A longer rod reaches farther and deeper water,
where more valuable fish may occur.  After a long quiet spell, the nearest fish
which can live at the hook's depth begins moving visibly toward it.  It must
still cross the water and occupy the hook's exact cell before it can bite, so
casting near fish matters.

Bite and winding
----------------

When BITE appears, press Enter to strike or Space to wait one turn.  A missed
moment lets the fish escape.  After a successful strike, press Up or keypad 8
once per turn to reel.  The fish may pull line back before it is landed.

A catch becomes an ordinary carried item which can be eaten or sold.  To feed
the village, carry fish into the General Store and press uppercase D (Donate
catch), then choose the catch and quantity.  Rain Minnows, Mudbellies, and
Glassfins contribute 1, 3, and 6 portions respectively.  Securing the first
20-portion emergency reserve makes Expedition Rations available from the
General Store.  The Fishing Shop keeper knows where a full-depth Yew Longline
was lost.  Check the visible map and message history before casting: the water
is not a pause screen.

Rain Minnows and Mudbellies give 1 XP when caught and another 1 XP when
donated. Glassfins give 2 XP for each. Donation XP counts fish, not portions;
giving a stack at once pays the same as donating its fish individually. These
small rewards continue after the larder unlock. Selling fish gives gold, not XP.

Cave pools
----------

In a side-view cave, stand securely on an air tile directly beside the pool.
The status line shows "Ctrl-C Fish" when the position is valid.  You cannot cast
while falling, climbing, hanging, or swimming.  Fishing actions use the cave's
normal action cadence, so nearby cave creatures continue to act; a catch which
does not fit in the pack falls onto the persistent cave tile at your feet.
