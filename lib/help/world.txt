The Known World and Travel
==========================

Ammerow's launch basin is a deliberately compact three-by-three surface.  The
village, roads, woods, lake and cave approaches are real local maps joined at
their edges.  Green arrows mark a physical route out of the current region.
Stepping onto a travel arrow crosses to its connected region and teaches
that direction of the route, regardless of your direction of approach.

The known-world screen
----------------------

Open the screen with '<' while on the surface, or choose Known world / travel
from the Enter menus.  Direction keys move its cursor one map cell.  '@' marks
your current surface region, 'v' marks the surface above your underground
location, '*' is a discovered region, and '?' remains unknown.

Enter travels to a selected discovered region only after its route has been
learned in the required direction.  Fast travel is refused while threatened,
restrained, affected by an unsafe temporary condition, or already being moved
by another effect.  It advances world time, hunger and light fuel.  It does not
simulate or animate every step of the journey.

Entrances and the Meridian
--------------------------

'>' uses a down entrance under the player.  The Meridian is the hundred-layer
descent that anchors the first release.  Satellite caves can hold different
rewards or connect to a side-view cave through a marked hole.  Ordinary trap
doors never count as a side-view entrance.

Cavewood Cavern is one top-down area. Its exits lead back to
Cavewood or down the shaft into the Deepwell's side-view caves. It has no
ordinary down staircases leading to lower regular dungeon floors.

Look names these entrances for their destinations. Cavewood Cavern's mossy
green rock and earthy floor contrast with the Meridian's cool slate walls.
Green Y trees on the surface can be walked through, but their foliage blocks
sight and missiles. Ordinary grey rock is still solid.

Only sites physically discovered by this character are named on the map.  The
small launch basin is a complete expedition boundary, not the claimed full
extent of Ammerow; later regions can attach through the same route system.
