
Ammerow Original Keyset Command Summary
=======================================

Escape cancels a prompt or clears a selected target before opening the in-game
menu.  Ctrl+C casts a fishing line while you stand
beside eligible water.  On the surface, '<' opens the known-world map; below
ground it uses an up staircase.  Enter also opens action and information menus,
including the same known-world screen.  See 'world.txt', 'fishing.txt', and
'spelunking.txt' for the context-specific controls.

The map camera follows you automatically, keeping nearby terrain clear of the
statistics and message panels even at map edges. No centring key is needed.

In top-down play, apostrophe (') immediately selects the nearest target;
it does not open a targeting cursor.  '*' opens interactive targeting.
Escape clears a retained target; press Escape again for the in-game menu.
While a shot is asking for a direction, '5' uses the retained target and
apostrophe selects and aims at the closest enemy.  Escape cancels that prompt.

In the original keyset, '.' asks for a direction and runs until something
interesting or unsafe is encountered; ',' waits in place and picks up.  The
roguelike keyset swaps those two keys.  Side-view caves do not use automatic
running: '.' waits there, and holding a direction requests repeated physical
steps.

^ followed by a letter is an abbreviation for pressing and releasing
the letter key with the control key also depressed.  You may also press
and release ^ and then press and release the letter key to activate the
same command in case your system intercepts the control plus key
combination and does not pass it on.  The autoexplore_commands option
modifies the '<', '>', and 'p' commands.  When that option is off (that
is the default), the commands act as described below.  When that option
is on, '<' or '>' will use the staircase at the player's location if it
is the appropriate kind of staircase or will move to the nearest known
staircase of the appropriate kind if the player is not already at that
kind of staircase.  'p' will move to the nearest unexplored location
when the autoexplore_commands option is on.
