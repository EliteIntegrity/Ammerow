Interface and Accessibility
===========================

F1 or ?
  Open this field guide from home, settings, the in-game menu, or play.

Home references
  Lore introduces the world, its peoples, and Classes & Ranks. Memorial lists
  scored expeditions. Help / Field Guide opens these practical instructions.
  Settings changes presentation; About holds the version, credits and licences.

Escape
  Open the small in-game menu during play.  It also backs out of menus and
  cancels temporary modes such as Look or fishing.

Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+2 / Ctrl+3
  Toggle top messages, left-hand statistics, and bottom messages.  These are
  transparent overlays: the playfield continues beneath them.

Mouse wheel or Ctrl+Plus / Ctrl+Minus / Ctrl+0
  Magnify, reduce, or reset the playfield.  Map zoom is independent of menu and
  message size, remains crisp, and also works in side-view caves.

Mouse in menus
  Moving the pointer highlights Home, Settings, and in-game menu choices.
  Click uses the highlighted choice; keyboard controls remain fully equivalent.

Character creation
  Press = on the origin, class, or attribute-method menu for birth options.
  Enter toggles the selected option; Y/N sets it and Escape returns.  S saves
  defaults, R restores saved defaults, and X resets factory defaults.  Birth
  options are locked after starting a run; experimental options remain labelled.
  Attribute allocation shows base values, origin/class modifiers, final values,
  point costs and live derived values.  R resets allocation.  In the roller,
  R rerolls and P swaps with the previous candidate; colours and +/- marks
  compare the final stats.  Stat effects have thresholds, not linear gains.

Ctrl+Alt+S
  Open settings.  Appearance controls theme, map font, ASCII treatment, and
  the ASCII/Hybrid playfield choice. Hybrid uses portrait sprites at 32 x 32
  or 64 x 64 detail, with ordinary ASCII terrain and fallback glyphs. Detail
  changes do not change map scale. Cards and shopkeepers remain coloured
  ASCII illustrations. Interface controls the non-map font,
  density, statistics and message overlays.  Sound and display/performance
  have their own pages.

F11 or Alt+Enter
  Toggle fullscreen.

Look and examine
  Press L, move one tile at a time with direction keys, and press Escape to
  clear the card.  The examined cell is visibly marked.  Side-view Peek is a
  different safety action described in 'spelunking.txt'.
  A selected combat target may retain its marker after an attack, but its
  examination card closes when you finish aiming. Use L to inspect it again.

Shops
  Highlight stock with the arrow keys. L examines it immediately; Enter opens
  its actions, including Examine. Use d to sell carried items. Shift+D also
  sells except at larder donation shops, where it donates a catch instead.
  The shop footer shows the available actions. If nothing is suitable to sell,
  the explanation stays above the stock until your next action.

Poison
  While poisoned, the statistics panel shows Poisoned beside the HP display,
  in addition to messages and the status line. It clears when poison ends.

Learning and using magic
------------------------

Formula, prayer, practice and ritual are the different realms' names for
book magic. Learning one does NOT make it a passive ability or activate it.

  G       Learn book magic (if your class has a study slot available).
  m       Use learned magic: choose its book, then the learned ability.
  b       Browse a book without using it (P in the roguelike keyset).

Carry the relevant book so it is available when you need it. Top-down play
also allows using a book on the floor at your feet; caves require it in your
pack. The list shows the required character level, Focus cost per attempt,
current failure chance and learning status. Descriptions are shown by default;
? hides or shows them. Arrow keys can inspect unlearned entries too, but Enter
or a letter only activates eligible magic. "Not learned" means you must first
leave this menu and study with G; being the right level is not enough.
Attempting magic spends action time and Focus, even if
concentration fails. With insufficient Focus you can risk overexertion, which
may make you faint or harm your Constitution. Cancelling a selection is free.

Remove Hunger is a one-off top-up to roughly half fed when you are below that
amount. It does nothing if you are already at least half fed, and does not slow
future digestion. Learn it, then use m when hungry; it must be used again when
needed. A temporary protection such as Resist Poison likewise needs to be
used, and then lasts only for its duration. By contrast, an equipped item's
Slow Digestion works automatically while worn; learning magic is unrelated.

S opens the origin/class abilities reference. It describes traits and their
conditions; it is not the menu for activating learned book magic.

Performance
-----------

The renderer redraws changed cells and caps weather animation work.  Turn
Visible weather off in Display / Performance on older or battery-powered
hardware.  Weather chooses itself automatically; this switch hides its visuals
without muting sound or changing gameplay.  Use Sound for audio controls.
Interface density and map zoom can be adjusted
independently, so a dense menu does not make the map tiny.

The map camera keeps the player (or active Look cursor) clear of a visible
statistics overlay.  It can reveal a blank margin at the map edge to do so;
terrain elsewhere still draws beneath the transparent overlays.

The terminal renderer remains the compatibility fallback.  Some infrequent
ancestral screens deliberately use it until they receive a native screen.
