# Ammerow compiled visual assets

The `FROGART3` files below this directory are compiled forms of separately
licensed Ammerow visual assets. They are not GPL-covered source or free asset
packs.

The plates are compiled from image-generated source illustrations made under
human direction. The same source illustrations supply the 32x32 and
64x64 Hybrid sprites; those sprites are not conversions of rendered ASCII
glyphs. Neither compilation nor sprite conversion changes the source
provenance or the separate asset terms below.
