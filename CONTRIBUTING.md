# Contributing

Discuss substantial contributions before starting them. Publication is not a
promise that every unsolicited contribution will be accepted.

Offer only work you have the right to contribute. Original code offered for
integration must be distributable under GPLv2 as part of Ammerow; contributors
retain copyright. Identify any additional compatible file-level terms and
third-party origins before integration. Do not introduce non-commercial,
no-forks or other GPL-incompatible restrictions.

Preserve inherited notices and attribute modifications without claiming the
whole inherited file. New dependencies require their source, version, licence
and acknowledgements. Separately licensed game assets are not available for
reuse merely because the program is open source.

Keep changes focused, data driven and proportionately tested. Preserve the
ordinary-glyph and synthesised-effect fallbacks. Follow
[building and testing](docs/building.md) and [data authoring](docs/data-authoring.md).
Commit editable authorities and generated outputs together.
