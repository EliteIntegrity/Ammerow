## Covered assets

The covered assets are:

- source images under `assets/monsters/`, `assets/shopkeepers/`,
  `assets/items/`, and `assets/terrain/`;
- the compiled `FROGART3` plates under `lib/art/`, which are transformed forms
  of those source images;
- the earlier Ammerow map-tile pages `lib/tiles/map-*.png`, retained for
  experimentation and not shipped in the current release, including their
  creature, equipment and terrain cells, and their source artwork under
  `assets/tiles/`;
- the Hybrid sprite sheets `lib/tiles/pixel-*.png` and their retained
  experimental masters `assets/experiments/portrait-pixels/tiles/pixel-*.png`,
  transformed from the separately licensed, image-generated source
  illustrations also used for the ASCII art;
- the authored sound-effect and home-music WAV files under
  `lib/sounds/ammerow/`, including its subdirectories;
- the official marketing and store artwork under `assets/marketing/`,
  including its retained source backgrounds and finished derivatives; and
- the official Windows application icon at `src/win/ammerow.ico` and its
  compiled form embedded in an official executable.

the GPL-covered source unless a file says otherwise. Third-party fonts and
